# Questions on Conditionals

<details>
<summary>1. Number Sign Checker
</summary>
Problem: Check whether a number is Positive, Negative, or Zero. Then, for positive and negative numbers, also print whether the number is Even or Odd.
(e.g., 7 - Positive, Odd | -4 - Negative, Even | 0 - Zero)

</details>

<details>
<summary>2. Traffic Light Action
</summary>
Problem: Print the action a driver should take based on the traffic light color: Red - Stop, Yellow - Get Ready, Green - Go. Print "Invalid signal" for any other color. Use a switch statement.

</details>

<details>
<summary>3. Greeting by Time of Day
</summary>
Problem: Print a greeting based on the hour (0-23): Good Morning (5-11), Good Afternoon (12-16), Good Evening (17-20), Good Night (21-23 and 0-4).

</details>

<details>
<summary>4. Largest of Three Numbers
</summary>
Problem: Given three numbers, find and print the largest one using if / else if / else and logical operators. Handle the case where two or more numbers are equal.

</details>

<details>
<summary>5. Shopping Discount
</summary>
Problem: Apply a discount based on the cart total: below 1000 - no discount, 1000 to 4999 - 10% discount, 5000 and above - 20% discount. Print the original amount, the discount amount, and the final amount to pay.

</details>

<details>
<summary>6. Triangle Type Checker
</summary>
Problem: Given three side lengths, check whether they can form a triangle (the sum of any two sides must be greater than the third). If valid, print whether it is Equilateral (all sides equal), Isosceles (two sides equal), or Scalene (no sides equal).

</details>

<details>
<summary>7. Simple Calculator
</summary>
Problem: Given two numbers and an operator (+, -, *, /, %), print the result using a switch statement. For division and remainder, print "Cannot divide by zero" if the second number is 0. Print "Invalid operator" for anything else.

</details>

<details>
<summary>8. Electricity Bill Calculator
</summary>
Problem: Calculate the electricity bill based on units consumed: first 100 units at 5 per unit, next 100 units (101-200) at 7 per unit, and every unit above 200 at 10 per unit.
(e.g., 250 units = 100 x 5 + 100 x 7 + 50 x 10 = 1700)

</details>

<details>
<summary>9. BMI Category
</summary>
Problem: Calculate BMI using weight (kg) and height (m) with the formula weight / (height x height), then print the category: Underweight (< 18.5), Normal (18.5-24.9), Overweight (25-29.9), Obese (30 and above).

</details>

<details>
<summary>10. ATM Withdrawal Check
</summary>
Problem: Given an account balance and a withdrawal amount, decide the result: "Invalid amount" if the amount is 0 or negative, "Amount must be a multiple of 100" if it is not divisible by 100, "Insufficient balance" if it exceeds the balance, otherwise print "Withdrawal successful" and the remaining balance.

</details>
